# AI for Good Lab Hackathon, United Arab Emirates (2026): requirements summary

## Task
Build and demonstrate an AI agent-based solution using a knowledge base created from use-case documentation and code, in domains such as healthcare, education, telecom and public services, adhering to country AI policies, standards and regulations, mapped to global best practices.

## Agent roles
Innovative AI solution; policy and legal adviser; institutional participant; multi-agent coordination with agents receiving different information and operating under different mandates.

## Scenarios and audit
Interactive error scenarios with events released progressively, information incomplete and uneven; audit logging and chain-of-thought tracing of decisions, reasoning and policy references.

## Evaluation
Knowledge base quality; agent capability; policy, strategy and standards gap identification with relevant global policy examples; auditability; alignment with ITU-T Y.3172 and the ITU AI Readiness Framework.
